Rent and housing

Utilities

Groceries

Saturdays are shopping days, not five locked $50 bills. This number is room you can spend if you need it. Type the real trip on This month — spend as little as you can. Blank Saturdays do not come out of a paycheck.

Credit cards

Credit is the limit on the card. Used is what is on it now. The percent is used ÷ credit. After you mark Sent, used drops by that payment.

Student loans

Enter what you owe, day 1, months, and the APR on the loan right now. The monthly amount is a normal amortizing payment (principal + interest over those months). If you refinance, type the new APR and it recalculates.

Car insurance

Monthly premium. Fixed if the bill is the same; Variable if you type the send on This month.

Gas

This is room per fill, not a locked weekly bill. Add a fill on This month when you actually pump. Spend under this if you can.

Car finance

October 2 is day 1 of the first payment. APR is the loan rate used to size the monthly payment over your months — not one month of interest sitting next to the term. Change APR if you refinance.

Other loans

Personal payments

Amount left is what you still owe a person. Mark Sent on This month and that payment comes off this balance. Pay $700, Settings shows $700 less.

Payoff plans

Payoff uses the fixed Settings amount, or this month’s contribution if the loan is variable.
